# Code of Conduct

AxilJS follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## In short
- Be respectful and welcoming to everyone, regardless of background or experience level.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- No harassment, insults, personal attacks, or publishing others' private information.
- Focus on what is best for the community and the project.

## Reporting
If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, email **tariq@syntaxility.dev**.
All reports are reviewed confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments, commits or
contributions, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone who violates this code.
